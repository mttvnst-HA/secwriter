The UFGS Facilities Guide Specifications (UFGS) is the primary page for the UFGS Master, hosted and managed by the National Institute of Building Sciences, Whole Building Design Guides (WBDG). The UFGS Master is a joint effort of the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE), the Naval Facilities Engineering Command (NAVFAC), and the Air Force Civil Engineering Command (AFCEC).  The UFGS Master is used for specifying construction for the military services.

From the UFGS Master web page, you can download the entire UFGS Master, specific Division(s), or individual Sections.  In addition, you can submit Criteria Change Requests (CCR) for technical and non-technical comments, suggestions, or recommendations.  Here you can access the following:

  • UFGS Master
  • Unified Master Reference List (UMRL)
  • UFGS Complete - Current UFGS Master in a single PDF document
  • UFGS Table of Contents available as a PDF document
  • UFGS Changes and Revisions - Current listing of changes for the current UFGS Master
  • UFGS Section Template - For submitting Criteria Change Requests (CCRs) only
  • UFGS Forms, Graphics, and Tables - Source of documents designed to accompany UFGS Master Sections in the following formats: Adobe PDF (.pdf), Microsoft Word (.docx), Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), CAD Drawings (compressed zip file), and various other file formats in zip format.
  • UFGS Table of Contents (PDF Document)
