Bracket Verification

The Bracket Verification Report is an essential diagnostic tool designed to identify and highlight any unresolved or mismatched brackets within the project's Sections, reminding the specifier to finalize the necessary choices. All errors identified in the Bracket Verification Report must be corrected before a Job reaches its final stage. Addressing these issues is a mandatory step to ensure your specifications are precise, complete, and fully compliant for submission.

The Bracket Verification Report (BRKTVER.RPT) lists all brackets remaining in the Sections that must be removed and replaced with text before a Job is complete.

To learn more about the Bracket Replacement tool, refer to the Bracket Replacement topic.

Resolving Discrepancies

When you encounter errors in this report, double-click on the listed Section Number to open the Section in the SI Editor. The cursor will automatically be placed at the exact location of the bracket issue.  To correct these, use the Bracket Replacement Tool. 

  • If a bracket was unintentionally left in the text, simply remove it.
  • If you have bracketed options remaining, you must choose an option or enter the required information, then delete the surrounding brackets.
