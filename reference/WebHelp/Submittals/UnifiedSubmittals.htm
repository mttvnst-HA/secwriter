The Unified Submittal Descriptions provide the essential standardized terminology and criteria for all project Submittals. Adopted by all agencies, this unified approach is critical for maintaining consistent technical content within specifications, facilitating accurate reviews, and ensuring overall project compliance.

Submittal Descriptions

The Tri-Services streamlined list of Submittal Descriptions that combines and simplifies Submittal Descriptions (SD) into eleven core categories:

SD-01  Preconstruction Submittals

SD-02  Shop Drawings

SD-03  Product Data

SD-04  Samples

SD-05  Design Data

SD-06  Test Reports

SD-07  Certificates

SD-08  Manufacturer's Instructions

SD-09  Manufacturer's Field Reports

SD-10 Operation and Maintenance Data

SD-11 Closeout Submittals

For more information about Submittals, refer to the 01 33 00 Submittal Procedures Section.

Backward compatibility

SpecsIntact differentiates between legacy and current Submittal Descriptions (SD), preventing the mixing of differing formats within a Job. Sections with current Submittal Descriptions are identified by an MTA tag within the Section Header. The software will continue to support Jobs and Masters, containing legacy Submittals. To make an older Section compliant with current Submittal Descriptions in a Job or local Master, use the SI Editor to change the SD numbers, update the Submittal Article format, and add the MTA tag.
