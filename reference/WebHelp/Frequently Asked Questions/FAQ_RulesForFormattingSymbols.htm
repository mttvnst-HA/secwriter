Can I Use Symbols In My Text?

To prevent potential software conflicts with command characters, spell out the symbols within the chart below rather than using their symbols. 

Symbol For
' foot
" inch
# pound
% percent
° degree
+ plus
- minus
+/- plus or minus
x by
/ per
@ at

The use of feet and inch symbols are acceptable when both are in a dimension (e.g., 8'-8") and within tables. Avoid or minimize the use of parentheses and quotation marks.

 When preparing UFGS Master Sections or editing specifications for construction projects, refer to the Unified Facilities Guide Specifications (UFGS) Format Standard (UFS 1-300-02). The UFGS format is based on the Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) SectionFormat/PageFormat™.
