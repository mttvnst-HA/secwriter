Are There Rules For Formatting Numbers In The Text?

You should follow these Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) standards when formatting numbers:

  • Spell out feet when no inches are used -- (8 feet)
  • Spell out inches when no feet are used -- (8 inches)
  • When using both feet and inches, symbols are acceptable and can be used in tables -- (3'-8")
  • A complete dimension must appear on one line; do not separate.
  • Standard Reference identifiers must appear on one line; do not separate -- (ASTM C144, CSA/ANSI Z21.22)
  • Use numbers whenever possible, and always for dimensions
  • When defining both quantity and dimensions, use the written word for quantity and the number for the dimension -- (three 1/2 inch holes)
  • Always use figures for degrees, percent, and money -- (50 degrees F (20 degrees C), 20 percent, $5.50)
  • Decimals should be expressed in figures -- (6.235)
  • For quantities less than one, a zero should be used before the decimal point -- (0.235)
  • To maintain uniformity throughout the specification, fractions should be typed using individual keys rather than the limited number of fraction keys provided on standard keyboards -- (1/4 and 1/2)
  • Omit unnecessary zeros in time and money references -- (9 p.m., $200)

 When preparing UFGS Master Sections or editing specifications for construction projects, refer to the Unified Facilities Guide Specifications (UFGS) Format Standard (UFS 1-300-02). The UFGS format is based on the Construction Specifications Institute (CSI) SectionFormat/PageFormat™.
