Encountering Security Warning when Publishing to PDF with Adobe PDF printer

This is due to an Adobe Acrobat update noted in the Adobe Knowledge Base, which only relates to Acrobat DC, Acrobat 2017, Adobe Acrobat 2017 and Adobe Acrobat 2015 Classic.

Solution

The Adobe Knowledge Base offers various solutions, but the one specifically compatible with SpecsIntact involves Acrobat Distiller, as detailed below. These steps are directly from Adobe's official documentation.

Proceed with Caution - Some users may require elevated privileges to modify the Registry settings and need assistance from a System Administrator.

To Fix The Registry Settings

  1. Close SpecsIntact and Adobe Acrobat
  2. In the Windows Search Bar, enter Regedit.exe, then press Enter
  3. In the User Account Control window, click Yes
  4. In the Registry Editor, navigate to HKEY_CURRENT_USER > Software > Adobe > Acrobat Distiller > DC > Preferences
  5. In Preferences, right-click on the bEnabledTrustedConversion binary key in the right pane and select Modify, then change its value to 01
  6. Close the Registry Editor
  7. Open SpecsIntact and proceed to Publish to PDF
