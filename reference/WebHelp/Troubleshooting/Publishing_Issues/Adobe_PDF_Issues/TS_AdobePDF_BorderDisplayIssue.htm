FAQs, Troubleshooting, and Technical Notes

Table Borders Do Not Display Correctly After Publishing

The issue you're seeing isn't a problem with SpecsIntact or how it publishes to PDF, whether you use its integrated SpecsIntact PDF printer or the Adobe PDF printer. This is solely an Adobe Acrobat display issue. If you adjust the zoom in Adobe Acrobat gradually, the borders will appear, but they'll vanish again if you zoom in or out too much. This won't affect how your Sections print.

Solution

Since this issue is confirmed to be an Adobe Acrobat display issue, you can try adjusting the display settings within Adobe Acrobat to potentially resolve it. 

Adobe Reader Or Adobe Acrobat

Once you apply the Adobe Acrobat display settings below, the missing table borders should display properly without having to zoom in/out.

  1. Open Adobe Reader or Acrobat, then perform one of the following:
    1. Select the Edit menu and select Preferences 
    2. Use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl+K
  2. In the Categories pane, select Page Display
  3. Below Rendering, deselect Enhance thin lines
  4. Click OK
