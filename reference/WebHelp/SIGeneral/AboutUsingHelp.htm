Glossary

You can view a comprehensive list of glossary terms by selecting the Glossary menu, or view a glossary term while reviewing a topic by clicking on the glossary term to expand or collapse the definition.  The glossary terms appear green and italicized within a topic.

 

Technical Support Information

Users are encouraged to visit the SpecsIntact Website's Support & Help Center for access to all of our User Tools, including Web-Based Help (containing Troubleshooting, Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs), Technical Notes, and Known Problems), eLearning Modules (video tutorials), and printable Guides.
